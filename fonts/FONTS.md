# Fonts

Put Myanmar TTF/OTF fonts in this folder. The app lists them automatically
in the subtitle/watermark font picker.

Recommended (free, OFL licensed):
- **Padauk** — https://github.com/silnrsi/font-padauk/releases
  (best Myanmar rendering; download Padauk-Regular.ttf and drop it here)

You can also upload fonts from the app's Font tab — no restart needed.
